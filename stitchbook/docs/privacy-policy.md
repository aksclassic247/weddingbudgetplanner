# StitchBook privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

StitchBook is a knitting and crochet app. It keeps your information on your device.

## What StitchBook stores

- Your projects, checklists, counters, notes and reminder times are saved on your device.
- Progress photos and PDF patterns you add are copied into the app's private storage on your device.
- If you export a backup, the backup file is saved wherever you choose. It contains your project data only, not photos or PDFs.

## What StitchBook does not do

- It does not create an account.
- It does not send your data to us or to anyone else. The app has no internet access.
- It does not show ads and does not use analytics or tracking.
- It does not use a camera.

## Permissions

- **Notifications.** Used only to show the daily reminders you turn on. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings.
- **Photos and PDFs.** Chosen through your phone's own picker. The app receives only the files you pick.

## Contacting us

If you write to us using the "Contact us" or "Report a problem" links, your email app opens and you send the message yourself. We receive only what you write. Your email address is handled by your email provider.

## Deleting your data

- Delete a project in the app to remove its data and files.
- Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your device.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.
