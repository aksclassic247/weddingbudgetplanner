# Store listing text (Google Play and App Store)

Use this text for both stores so the description matches what the app does. Do not add features here that the app does not have.

## Short description

Learn knitting and crochet step by step. Track your projects, rows and repeats.

## Full description

StitchBook helps you learn and follow knitting and crochet projects.

- Step-by-step tutorials for knitting and crochet, with a stitch abbreviations guide.
- Patterns for every skill level, with a search box and filters.
- A checklist for each round, and a "Check my count" tool that tells you whether your count matches the pattern.
- Row counters with optional targets, and repeat counters that count pattern repeats for you.
- Daily reminders to pick up a project.
- Progress photos and your own PDF patterns, kept on your phone.
- A shaping calculator for increases and decreases, and a yarn estimate that tells you how many balls to buy.
- Export and restore a backup file to move your projects to another device.

Free, with no ads and no subscription.

What StitchBook does not do:
- It does not turn photos into patterns.
- It has no AI chat.
- The sample patterns were drafted with AI help and are not yet test-made. Check every count before you start, and tell us if something is wrong.

## Privacy notes for the store forms

- No account is needed.
- Projects, photos and PDFs stay on the device. Only a backup file you choose to export leaves the app.
- The app asks for notification permission only if you turn a reminder on.
- The app does not use a camera.
